Value & pricing

Selfix Life is free for personal journaling today. There is no paid wall to open your book — just a calm sign-in so your journal can stay private and travel with you. Features may evolve; if pricing ever changes, we will say so clearly first.

Data privacy & storage

After sign-in and email verification, your pixels, panels, notes, and settings sync to Firebase Cloud Firestore under your account. Your browser may also keep temporary state in LocalStorage on this device. Private notes are AES-encrypted on your device before they are written to the cloud.

The Insights Logbook can save or print reports from your real entries — a soft way to keep patterns and reflections beyond the screen. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

Core features

  • Year in Pixels — paint each day as a soft pastel square and watch your year become a readable mosaic.
  • Mood tracking — gentle color levels and optional chips for how the day felt.
  • Sleep tracking — rest and overnight patterns with calm visual clarity.
  • Habit tracking — hydration, focus, workouts, reading, and other daily rhythms.
  • Daily notes — private per-tracker reflections beside each day.

Why it feels different

Selfix Life is built for reflection without pressure — soft rose mornings, midnight evenings, and an open-book layout that remembers you across devices. Select a day, color it, reflect — no cold dashboards required.

FAQ

Is Selfix Life free?

Yes. It is currently free for personal journaling. Create an account to open your book.

How is my data stored?

Cloud sync to Firestore after verified sign-in, LocalStorage for temporary browser state, and on-device AES encryption for private notes before cloud write.

Can I continue on another device?

Yes — sign in with the same verified account and your synced journal follows you.

Can I keep a report of my insights?

Yes. Insights reports can be saved as text or printed as PDF from your logged entries.

Try as Guest & read the guides

Open Selfix Life and choose Try as Guest to explore the full Year in Pixels journal on your device without creating an account. Guest entries stay local until you sign in to save and sync. Or read calm journaling guides on our Blog — covering mood tracking, sleep patterns, habits, and finding your calm space.
